Privacy Policy · Effective November 9, 2026

CONTAIN: AI Strategy Puzzle ("the app") is made by Gary Gallagher ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects and why. We keep it short because we collect very little.

No accounts

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.

Stored on your device

Your progress, settings, stars, purchases and ECHO's notes about your play style (such as which side of the board you favor) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Live AI features (ECHO Unleashed)

When live lines or ECHO's voice are used, the app sends a short, anonymous game summary (for example: level number, number of moves, and how predictable your moves were) to our server. Our server uses Anthropic to write ECHO's line and ElevenLabs to generate its voice. The summary contains no personal information and is not used to identify you. We do not keep it after the response is sent, apart from short-lived technical logs for abuse prevention.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and subscriptions; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Advertising

The free version may show ads from an ad network. Ad networks may use device identifiers to show and measure ads. On iOS you control tracking with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Buying Remove Ads or ECHO Unleashed turns ads off.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can use the whole game offline. To ask a question or request deletion of any data related to you, email northlandai@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.

How to play

Tap an empty hex to drop a firewall. After each of your moves, ECHO steps one tile toward the edge of the board. Surround ECHO so it has no path out and you win. Reach the edge and it escapes.

Restoring purchases

Open STORE on the title screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Freeze packs are consumable and are not restored.

Managing ECHO Unleashed

ECHO Unleashed renews automatically until cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To cancel, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Performance

Open the gear icon on the title screen and choose a graphics mode: Auto, Smooth, Beautiful or Battery Saver.

Resetting progress

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
